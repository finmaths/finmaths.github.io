Syllabus outcomes and descriptors

Source: NSW Education Standards Authority (NESA). (2022). Mathematics K–10 syllabus, Stage 5, Financial mathematics A. The outcome and content wording below is quoted from the syllabus. Working Mathematically indicators in each lesson are written by the teacher and shown in italics.

Outcomes

Code Outcome
MA5-FIN-C-01 solves financial problems involving simple interest, earning money and spending money
MAO-WM-01 develops understanding and fluency in mathematics through exploring and connecting mathematical concepts, choosing and applying mathematical techniques to solve problems, and communicating their thinking and reasoning coherently and clearly

Content and where it is taught

Solve problems involving earning money

Syllabus content Lesson
Solve problems involving wages given an hourly rate of pay including penalty rates for overtime, weekends and public holidays 1, 2
Calculate earnings from non-wage sources exploring commission, piecework and royalties 1
Calculate weekly, fortnightly, monthly and yearly earnings assuming 1 year = 52 weeks 3
Calculate leave loading by finding a percentage of eligible normal pay 3
Investigate sources of published tables or online calculators and use these to calculate the weekly, fortnightly or monthly tax to be deducted from a worker's pay under the Australian Pay-As-You-Go (PAYG) taxation system 3
Determine annual taxable income by exploring allowable deductions and current tax rates 3b (outline)
Calculate net earnings after deductions and taxation 3

Solve problems involving simple interest

Syllabus content Lesson
Establish and use the formula I = Prn to find simple interest where I = simple interest, P = principal, r = interest rate per time period and n = number of time periods 4–6
Apply the simple interest formula to solve problems related to investing money at simple interest rates, both algebraically and graphically 4–6

Solve problems involving spending money

Syllabus content Lesson
Calculate the cost of buying items on terms, by paying an initial deposit and making regular repayments 7
Examine payment options involving buy now, pay later and investigate the costs associated with these schemes for purchasing goods 8
Examine the principles behind short-term loans involving small dollar amounts and compare borrowing costs associated with using these products 9
